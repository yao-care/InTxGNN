## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Piperaquine is a bisquinoline antimalarial thought to act by inhibiting heme detoxification in the parasite's digestive vacuole, a mechanism shared with chloroquine. Its efficacy in malaria is established, and it is mainly used together with dihydroartemisinin (DHA-piperaquine).

*P. vivax* belongs to the same genus as the parasites it is already approved against, so this is closer to an extension of the approved indication than true repurposing. Piperaquine's long elimination half-life gives post-treatment protection against early relapse. However, it has **no activity against the dormant liver stage (hypnozoites)**. Radical cure of vivax therefore requires an 8-aminoquinoline (primaquine or tafenoquine) given alongside it.

Other predictions (echinococcosis, mastocytosis, nocardiosis, juvenile arthritis, enterobiasis) rest on the model score alone, with no clinical evidence and no plausible mechanistic link.

## Cytotoxicity

Not applicable: piperaquine is an antimalarial, not an antineoplastic drug.

## Safety Considerations

- **QT prolongation**: Piperaquine prolongs the QT interval, so QTc monitoring is advised.
- **G6PD testing**: Required before using an 8-aminoquinoline (primaquine or tafenoquine) for radical cure.
- **Special populations and regions**: Take care in pregnancy and in regions with chloroquine-resistant vivax.

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available in the compiled data, and no drug interactions were found. Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Multiple completed Phase 3 trials and randomised studies support DHA-piperaquine in vivax malaria, and the drug is already marketed in India for malaria. Piperaquine does not clear the dormant liver stage, so it is a blood-stage partner and not a stand-alone radical cure. All other predictions (including cystic echinococcosis, which has the same top score) are unsupported by trial evidence and should remain on Hold.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings, contraindications and approved indication text for the Indian product (Fal-DP)
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- A protocol pairing piperaquine with an 8-aminoquinoline, with G6PD testing and QTc monitoring
- Review of local chloroquine- and piperaquine-resistance patterns and of use in pregnancy
