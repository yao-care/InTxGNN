## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, trastuzumab binds HER2 and blocks its signalling. Its established effects include antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) and inhibition of downstream PI3K/AKT signalling.

HER2-positive breast cancer frequently co-occurs with hormone receptor (progesterone/estrogen receptor) positivity. Patients with PR-positive, HER2-positive tumours therefore fall within the population already treated with trastuzumab. Combining HER2 blockade with endocrine therapy is a recognised strategy in this group.

This should be viewed as an approved-indication subtype rather than a truly new indication. Benefit is expected only in confirmed HER2-positive patients. PR-positive tumours that are HER2-negative (for example luminal A) are not expected to respond.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 94 interactions were identified in the compiled interaction data. Examples of the **Major** level include deferiprone, samarium (153Sm) lexidronam, adalimumab and baricitinib. **Moderate** examples include paclitaxel, warfarin, dicoumarol, alemtuzumab, azathioprine, zidovudine, chloramphenicol and several vaccines (tetanus toxoid, anthrax).
- **Cardiac toxicity**: The literature compiled for this report (PMID 17687157) discusses trastuzumab-related cardiotoxicity and questions whether it is fully reversible. Cardiac monitoring is therefore an important consideration.

Warnings and contraindications from the package insert were not available. Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
A completed Phase 3 RCT (NCT01275677, n=3270) and multiple randomised studies support trastuzumab-based treatment in HER2-positive breast cancer, which includes PR-positive patients. Because this is a subtype of an already approved indication, benefit applies only to confirmed HER2-positive disease.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Restrict use to confirmed HER2-positive patients; there is no support for HER2-negative PR-positive disease
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Indian regulator (currently missing)
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- A cardiac monitoring plan (baseline and periodic LVEF assessment)
- Review of the Major drug interactions before combination use
- Dedicated evidence for the PR-positive/HER2-positive subgroup, since the Phase 3 trial data mainly cover the broader HER2-positive population
