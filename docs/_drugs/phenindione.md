## Safety Considerations

- **Key warnings from the literature**: Phenindione has been linked to severe immuno-allergic reactions, including acute hepatitis, acute renal failure and acute bone marrow failure. A severe drug sensitivity reaction was reported as early as 1954. Skin necrosis is a rare but occasionally lethal complication of anticoagulants of this type. Bleeding is the main complication of oral anticoagulants.
- **Regulatory context**: One pharmacology source states phenindione was discontinued in some markets because of a higher incidence of severe adverse effects than warfarin.
- **Drug interactions**: The only interaction recorded is its pharmacological target, VKORC1. No drug-drug interaction levels are available. Oral anticoagulants as a class have many clinically significant interactions (see PMID 8037888).

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available. Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.
