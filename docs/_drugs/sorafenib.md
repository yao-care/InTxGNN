# Sorafenib: From Hepatocellular Carcinoma to Liposarcoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Sorafenib is a multikinase inhibitor originally used to treat hepatocellular carcinoma, renal cell carcinoma and thyroid carcinoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **liposarcoma**,
with **1 clinical trial** (a Phase 2 study in mixed soft tissue sarcomas) and **8 publications** currently bearing on this direction. Most of that evidence is indirect or preclinical.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, sorafenib is a multikinase inhibitor that acts on the RAF/MEK/ERK pathway and on VEGFR and PDGFR. Its efficacy in hepatocellular and renal cell carcinoma is established, and mechanistically it may be applicable to soft tissue sarcomas.

Angiogenesis and MAPK signalling are relevant in soft tissue sarcomas. Preclinical work shows that sorafenib inhibits growth and MAPK signalling in sarcoma models, including dedifferentiated liposarcoma cell lines.

The mechanistic fit is only partial, however. Liposarcoma is typically driven by MDM2/CDK4 amplification rather than by sorafenib's targets, and activity in sarcomas appears to depend on histology. The prediction should therefore be treated as a research hypothesis, not an established opportunity.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only direct clinical evidence is a single-arm Phase 2 trial in mixed soft tissue sarcoma histologies. It is not liposarcoma-specific, and the mechanistic fit is only partial, since liposarcoma is driven mainly by MDM2/CDK4. The score is high (99.82%), but this is a model prediction and does not substitute for histology-specific efficacy data. The idea is worth keeping as a research question, not as a development candidate.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Liposarcoma-subtype efficacy data extracted from the Phase 2 SWOG S0505 study (PMID 21751200).
- Mechanism of action data (MOA) from DrugBank.
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the India regulator, currently a blocking gap for safety screening.
- A comparison against established liposarcoma options such as trabectedin and CDK4/MDM2-targeted agents.
- A safety plan for patients with cardiovascular or haematological risk factors.
