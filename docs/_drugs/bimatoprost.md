## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

According to the Mechanism of Action section of the US FDA-approved prescribing information for bimatoprost: "Bimatoprost, a prostaglandin analog, is a synthetic structural analog of prostaglandin with ocular hypotensive activity. Bimatoprost is believed to lower IOP in humans by increasing outflow of aqueous humor through both the trabecular meshwork (conventional) and uveoscleral routes (unconventional)." ([DailyMed: Durysta (bimatoprost) prescribing information, Allergan, Inc.](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3f59da84-0bcc-4c84-b3e2-e215681ef341), label version 2024-10-16; accessed 2026-11-19.) Bimatoprost is known as a prostamide F2-alpha analog that acts on prostanoid FP/prostamide receptor signalling and prolongs the hair growth (anagen) phase.

No plausible pathway links this mechanism to a dental or periodontal malformation syndrome. Such syndromes are congenital structural conditions, and a topical eye-drop prostamide is not expected to alter them. The high score is a model-based prediction only, with no supporting trial or literature evidence, so the prediction should be treated as a hypothesis with no demonstrated rationale.
