# Bimatoprost: From Open-Angle Glaucoma to Malformation Syndrome with Odontal and/or Periodontal Component

## One-Sentence Summary

Bimatoprost is a prostamide F2-alpha analogue eye drop, originally used for open-angle glaucoma and ocular hypertension (and also for eyelash hypotrichosis).
The TxGNN model ranks **malformation syndrome with odontal and/or periodontal component** as its top prediction, but **no clinical trials and no publications** support it, so this is a model-only signal.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Bimatoprost is a prostamide F2-alpha analogue. It lowers intraocular pressure and also prolongs the hair-growth (anagen) phase, which explains its eyelash use.

No plausible mechanistic link to a dental or periodontal malformation syndrome was identified. Such syndromes are congenital structural conditions, and a topical ophthalmic prostamide is not expected to affect them. The high score appears to be a graph-based artefact rather than a pharmacologically grounded signal. Similarity to the original indication has not been assessed.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction has no trials, no publications and no identifiable mechanism (L5), so it is not a credible repurposing candidate on current data.

A separate prediction in the same list, **alopecia** (model rank 8), is far better supported. It has several completed Phase 2 trials of topical bimatoprost in androgenetic alopecia (NCT01325337, NCT01904721, NCT01325350; roughly 240 to 310 participants each) and multiple reviews. No confirmatory Phase 3 trial exists, and the Phase 2 outcome data were not available for this report. I recommend evaluating that indication as a separate candidate.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- A rationale showing any biological link to the predicted syndrome (none identified so far)
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the India licence holders
- If the alopecia lead is pursued: Phase 2 efficacy results, a confirmatory trial plan, and a review of scalp-use safety and formulation
