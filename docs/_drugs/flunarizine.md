## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Flunarizine is a selective calcium entry blocker, acting on T-type and L-type calcium channels. Pharmacology data compiled for this report also show activity at the dopamine D2 receptor and at the Cav3.1, Cav3.2 and Cav3.3 T-type channels. It also has antihistaminic (H1) properties. Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not available in the source record, so this explanation rests on general pharmacological knowledge.

These actions plausibly reduce cortical spreading depression and neuronal hyperexcitability, and stabilize cerebrovascular tone. These are central mechanisms in migraine. The very high TxGNN score is consistent with this mechanistic rationale.

In practice, this prediction largely reflects existing use. Flunarizine is already used for migraine prophylaxis in many countries, though it is not FDA-approved. The Indian license records compiled for this report do not list approved indication text, so the formal labelled indication in India could not be confirmed.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the package insert for safety information.

Pharmacology data compiled for this report show flunarizine acts on the dopamine D2 receptor and on T-type calcium channels. No formal drug-drug interaction ratings are available. A Phase 1 study (NCT00752466) examined the pharmacokinetic interaction between flunarizine and topiramate, but its results are not summarized here.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Flunarizine has a long history of use in migraine prophylaxis, supported by controlled studies, reviews and guideline-level literature, and several head-to-head trials are completed or planned. However, most listed trials are small, open-label, or not yet reporting results. There are no completed Phase 3 RCTs among them, and package-insert safety data are missing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Indian license holders, which are currently missing and block safety screening
- Approved indication text for the Indian registrations
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data from DrugBank
- Results from the completed head-to-head trials (e.g., NCT02639598, NCT03712917)
- A safety monitoring plan for specific populations such as children
