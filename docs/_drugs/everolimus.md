## Everolimus: From Renal Cell Carcinoma to Liposarcoma
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Everolimus is an mTOR inhibitor originally used for renal cell carcinoma, breast cancer and tuberous sclerosis-related conditions.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **liposarcoma**, supported by **1 Phase 2 clinical trial** (combination with ribociclib) and **4 publications**, only one of which reports clinical results.
Evidence is limited for this top-ranked prediction. Among the other predictions, unclassified renal cell carcinoma has the strongest evidence.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, everolimus is an mTOR inhibitor (it blocks mTORC1). The PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway drives cell growth and is commonly overactive in renal cell carcinoma, which is why everolimus has proven efficacy there.

Dedifferentiated liposarcoma is characterized by CDK4/MDM2 amplification. Combining everolimus with the CDK4/6 inhibitor ribociclib blocks both the cell cycle and the mTOR growth pathway, which may limit compensatory signaling. The two drugs have shown synergistic growth inhibition in multiple tumor models.

This rationale is plausible but so far rests on a single-arm Phase 2 trial with no randomized comparator. Because everolimus was given with ribociclib, its individual contribution cannot be isolated.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
For liposarcoma, the only direct evidence is one single-arm Phase 2 trial of everolimus combined with ribociclib, so everolimus's own contribution is unknown. The licence data also lack package insert safety information. Unclassified renal cell carcinoma, by contrast, has stronger Phase 2 evidence and could be considered Proceed with Guardrails, though everolimus is already approved for RCC.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Full results of the ribociclib plus everolimus trial (NCT03114527, PMID 37967116), including response rate and progression-free survival in the liposarcoma arm
- Randomized or comparative data separating everolimus's effect from ribociclib's
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Package insert warnings, contraindications and approved indications from the India regulatory source
- A safety monitoring plan covering hematologic, metabolic, hepatic and pulmonary risks, and a drug interaction review for CYP3A4-related agents
