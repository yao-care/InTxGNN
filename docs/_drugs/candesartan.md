## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Candesartan blocks the angiotensin II type 1 (AT1) receptor, which is expressed in vascular and neural tissue. Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the compiled data, but several mechanisms have been proposed for migraine prevention. These include modulation of the renin-angiotensin system in cerebrovascular and trigeminovascular tissue, reduced neuroinflammation and oxidative stress, lower susceptibility to cortical spreading depression, and modulation of sympathetic tone and vascular reactivity.

Several guideline-oriented reviews list candesartan as a preventive option for migraine, and a recent scoping review (2025) examined its use for this purpose. This is consistent with the high prediction score. Direct trial data now exist in the target disease, so the prediction is supported by clinical studies and not by model output alone.
