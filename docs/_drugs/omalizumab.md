## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. From the drug's known pharmacology, omalizumab binds free IgE and reduces FcεRI expression on mast cells and basophils. This dampens allergic airway inflammation.

That mechanism could plausibly help allergic or asthma-related bronchitis. However, no study in the data directly tests omalizumab in bronchitis. The linked literature deals with asthma, asthma-COPD overlap and smoking-related airway disease. The mechanistic argument is therefore a hypothesis, not demonstrated benefit. Because no cited study addresses bronchitis directly, the evidence level is L5.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: Eight interaction records were found. One is rated Moderate (omacetaxine mepesuccinate). Six are rated Minor: zinc sulfate, zinc acetate, zinc gluconate, zinc chloride, echinacea and vitamin E. The remaining record is pharmacological and lists the drug's target, IgE heavy constant epsilon (IGHE).
- A published report (PMID 32377770) describes adrenal insufficiency as a contraindication for omalizumab in mast cell activation disease, because of the risk of serum sickness.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction score is high, but no cited study directly examines omalizumab in bronchitis. The one related trial is tiny and its condition is unclear, and the literature concerns asthma and other airway diseases. The evidence is model prediction only (L5).

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- A well-designed trial of omalizumab in a clearly defined bronchitis population, for example allergic or eosinophilic bronchitis
- Detailed mechanism-of-action data
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the India label
- A review of the drug interaction findings for the intended patients
