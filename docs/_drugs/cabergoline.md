## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Cabergoline acts by stimulating dopamine D2 receptors. The data compiled for this report contain no further detail on its mechanism of action.

No plausible mechanistic link between D2 receptor agonism and this syndrome can be established from the available data. The only support is the model's prediction score of 0.9906. This score is a model ranking and should not be read as a probability of clinical benefit.

The prediction is therefore a hypothesis only. It would need independent biological support before any clinical interpretation.
