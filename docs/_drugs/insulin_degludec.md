# Insulin Degludec: From Diabetes Mellitus to Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus

## One-Sentence Summary

Insulin degludec is an ultra-long-acting basal insulin analogue, originally used to treat diabetes mellitus and diabetic ketoacidosis.
The TxGNN model predicts it is effective for **Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus**, supported by **40 clinical trials** (including several completed Phase 3 trials) and **20 publications** (including multiple RCTs and systematic reviews).
This is effectively an on-label use rather than true repurposing.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, insulin degludec is a long-acting basal insulin analogue that replaces deficient endogenous insulin by activating the insulin receptor. Its half-life is about 25 hours and its duration of action exceeds 42 hours, giving a flat, stable glucose-lowering profile.

Type 1 diabetes is the core indication for insulin therapy, because people with this condition cannot produce enough insulin. The original indication (diabetes mellitus) already covers it, so the high TxGNN score is consistent with the established mechanism. Degludec has also been studied directly in type 1 diabetes in switching studies, pregnancy, exercise and paediatric populations.

Other predicted indications (autoimmune oophoritis, opsismodysplasia, thiamine-responsive dysfunction syndrome, focal stiff limb syndrome, classic stiff person syndrome) have no clinical trials or literature. Their links come from knowledge-graph proximity or comorbid diabetes, not from a pharmacological rationale, so they are rated **Hold** (L5) and not evaluated further here.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 351 interactions were recorded. Most listed examples are moderate:
  - Other glucose-lowering drugs: metformin, pioglitazone, alogliptin, albiglutide (additive hypoglycaemia risk is the likely concern)
  - Beta-agonists and sympathomimetics: salbutamol, formoterol, epinephrine, phenylephrine, pseudoephedrine
  - Corticosteroids, hormones and others: hydrocortisone, estradiol, levonorgestrel, norethisterone, hydrochlorothiazide, doxycycline, ethanol, acebutolol, alpelisib
  - Topical hydrocortisone and topical alclometasone are rated minor.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Several completed Phase 3 RCTs and multiple systematic reviews support degludec in type 1 diabetes, and the product is already marketed in India. Because this is on-label use, the question is mainly confirmation and safety management, not new efficacy.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Indian regulator, which are currently missing and block safety screening
- Detailed mechanism of action data, to complete the mechanistic analysis
- A hypoglycaemia monitoring plan, with attention to interactions with other glucose-lowering drugs
- Approved indication wording for the four Indian registrations, to confirm type 1 diabetes is explicitly covered
