## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, polyvinyl alcohol is a film-forming, water-retaining polymer used as an ocular lubricant. Its efficacy in dry eye comes from stabilising the tear film and retaining moisture on the surface of the eye.

A plausible but unverified link is that the same film-forming and hydrating behaviour could act as a topical occlusive or emollient on skin. This could temporarily improve hydration in scaling disorders and reduce water loss through the skin.

This link is weak. Congenital ichthyosiform erythroderma is a genetic keratinisation disorder, and a moisturising film would at best relieve symptoms rather than treat the underlying disease. The drug's established use is ocular, not dermatological.

Other predictions from the model follow the same pattern, all with no supporting studies:

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction is supported only by the TxGNN model score (Evidence Level L5), with no clinical trials or publications. The proposed mechanism is indirect and symptomatic, and the drug is only registered as an eye drop in India.
