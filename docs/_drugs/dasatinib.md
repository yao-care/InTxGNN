## Dasatinib: SRC/multi-kinase inhibitor → Ewing sarcoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Dasatinib is an oral multi-targeted kinase inhibitor that blocks BCR-ABL and SRC-family kinases. The TxGNN model predicts it may be useful for **Ewing sarcoma**, but the support so far comes mostly from laboratory studies. There are **2 clinical trials** (one terminated with 7 patients, one broad sarcoma trial) and **10 listed publications**, and a review of the sarcoma phase 2 study reports that single-agent dasatinib failed in Ewing sarcoma.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on published information, dasatinib is a kinase inhibitor that acts on BCR-ABL, SRC-family kinases, c-KIT and PDGFR.

Several laboratory studies show that SRC signalling drives invasion, migration and invadopodia formation in Ewing sarcoma cells. In sarcoma cell lines, dasatinib inhibited migration and invasion and induced apoptosis in bone sarcoma cells that depend on SRC. The FAK-SRC axis has also been proposed as a treatment target in Ewing sarcoma and related tumours.

The clinical picture is less encouraging. A review notes that single-agent dasatinib failed in Ewing sarcoma and rhabdomyosarcoma in a phase 2 study of advanced sarcomas. A recent single-centre case series looked at the RIST regimen (rapamycin, irinotecan, dasatinib, temozolomide) in relapsed Ewing sarcoma, which suggests a possible role only as part of combinations. None of the cited studies directly demonstrates a clinical benefit of dasatinib in Ewing sarcoma, so the evidence is capped at L5 despite the consistent mechanistic rationale.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The mechanistic rationale (SRC dependence of Ewing sarcoma) is consistent in the laboratory, but no cited study directly shows clinical benefit of dasatinib in Ewing sarcoma, and a review reports that single-agent dasatinib failed in this tumour type. The prediction therefore remains a research question.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Ewing sarcoma–specific outcome data from NCT00464620
- Evaluation of dasatinib within combination regimens (e.g., RIST) in controlled studies
- Mechanism of action data and package insert warnings and contraindications
- A cardiac, haematological and drug-interaction monitoring plan for a paediatric and young-adult population
