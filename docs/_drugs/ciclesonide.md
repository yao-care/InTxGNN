## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general pharmacology, ciclesonide is an inhaled corticosteroid prodrug. Esterases activate it to des-ciclesonide, a glucocorticoid receptor agonist with anti-inflammatory activity.

Corticosteroids, applied to the skin or given systemically, are an established treatment for atopic eczema. A glucocorticoid-mediated anti-inflammatory effect is therefore mechanistically plausible.

There are important limits. No trials or literature were found for ciclesonide in this condition. The supplied data show no skin formulation or skin-specific pharmacology for ciclesonide. The score is a model prediction only. Two other entries in the prediction list, "dermatitis, atopic" (0.9973) and "atopic eczema", describe essentially the same disease, so they should be treated as one candidate rather than as independent signals.
