## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications (from the knowledge graph, not label text)**: hypertension, epilepsy, anxiety and neurotic disorders, porphyria, kidney disease, liver disease, gallbladder disease, diabetes mellitus and hyperglycaemia, hypertriglyceridaemia, hypercalcaemia, hypothyroidism, systemic lupus erythematosus, asthma, and female breast carcinoma.
- **Drug Interactions**: 159 interactions were identified. The ones listed here are all moderate and mostly involve antidiabetic agents (acarbose, metformin, dapagliflozin, linagliptin, insulin products, GLP-1 agonists such as exenatide, dulaglutide, lixisenatide and albiglutide), plus aprepitant, clotrimazole, metreleptin, and prucalopride.

Package insert warnings were not available; please refer to the package insert for full safety information.
