## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Felodipine is, however, a known dihydropyridine L-type calcium channel blocker with vasodilatory activity. Its use in hypertension is recorded in the knowledge graph.

In theory, a vasodilator could counter hypoxic pulmonary vasoconstriction, the key mechanism in pulmonary hypertension due to lung disease. This is the basis of the TxGNN prediction.

The link is weak, though. None of the retrieved publications studied the pulmonary vasculature or pulmonary hypertension. They cover hypoxia or anoxia effects in rat kidney tubules, human atrial muscle, cerebral vessels, and neurons. Calcium channel blockers are also generally not recommended for this type of pulmonary hypertension, because they can inhibit hypoxic vasoconstriction and worsen ventilation-perfusion matching and gas exchange. The evidence level is therefore L5, because no cited study directly addresses this disease.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests only on a high model score. There are no clinical trials and no study of felodipine in pulmonary hypertension. The literature is indirect preclinical work, and calcium channel blockers are generally not recommended for pulmonary hypertension due to lung disease because of the risk of worsened gas exchange.

Other predicted indications are no stronger:
- Pulmonary hypertension with unclear multifactorial mechanism, malignant hypertensive renal disease, and Braddock syndrome have no trials or literature (L5).
- Chronic pulmonary heart disease has three older, small, indirect hemodynamic studies, including one in severe COPD (PMID 2838319). None of them shows a clinical outcome benefit, so evidence for this disease also remains at L5 under the rule that requires direct study of the disease itself.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Retrieve the package insert warnings and contraindications (currently a blocking data gap).
- Obtain mechanism of action data.
- Run preclinical studies in pulmonary vascular models of hypoxic pulmonary hypertension.
- Review the safety of worsened gas exchange and ventilation-perfusion mismatch before any clinical study.
