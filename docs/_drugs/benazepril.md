## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data for benazepril is not available in the data compiled for this report. Benazepril belongs to the ACE inhibitor class. These drugs block the renin-angiotensin system, which lowers blood pressure and reduces pressure inside the kidney's filtering units (glomeruli), as well as protein loss in the urine. ACE inhibitors have an established kidney-protective role in chronic kidney disease. That gives indirect plausibility for a condition in which severe high blood pressure damages the kidney.

There are important caveats. Malignant hypertension with kidney involvement is an acute, severe condition. In this setting, an ACE inhibitor can cause excessive blood pressure drops or acute kidney injury if it is not carefully monitored. No trials or literature were found for benazepril in this disease. The support is therefore indirect, from the drug class and the model prediction.
