## Safety Considerations

- **Contraindications (from the knowledge graph, not label text):** The TxGNN knowledge graph links sumatriptan to contraindications in the following groups. These need to be confirmed against the package insert.
  - Cardiovascular: hypertension, coronary artery disease, myocardial infarction, congestive heart failure, peripheral vascular disease, and other heart disease
  - Cerebrovascular: stroke, cerebral infarction, and other cerebrovascular disorders
  - Hepatic and renal: liver failure, liver disease, and kidney disease
  - Other: epilepsy, diabetes mellitus, diabetic ketoacidosis, substance abuse or dependence, blood coagulation disease, obesity, hemoglobinopathy, and anaemia
- **Drug Interactions:** 114 interactions are recorded. Major-level interactions include:
  - Serotonergic or appetite-suppressant agents: dexfenfluramine, fenfluramine, lorcaserin, sibutramine
  - 5-HT3 antagonist antiemetics: dolasetron, granisetron, ondansetron, palonosetron
  - Moderate-level interactions include morphine and liposomal morphine.
- **Subtype-specific caution:** Triptans have historically carried caution labelling for basilar-type and hemiplegic migraine because of theoretical vasoconstriction risk.

Package insert warnings and contraindications were not available for this report. Please refer to the package insert for full safety information.
