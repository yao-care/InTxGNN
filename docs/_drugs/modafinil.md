## Modafinil: Insomnia (disease)
{: .text-alpha }

## One-Sentence Summary

Modafinil is a wake-promoting medicine originally used to treat narcolepsy and related sleep/wake and central nervous system conditions.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **insomnia**, but the supporting evidence is thin: **2 clinical trials** (one terminated with only 2 participants, one small completed study) and no publications that directly test modafinil for insomnia.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Modafinil is a wake-promoting agent that is generally understood to act through dopamine transporter inhibition and effects on wake-promoting brain circuits. Its efficacy in excessive daytime sleepiness is established, and it is mechanistically aimed at that symptom.

The very high TxGNN score comes from the TxGNN model (deep learning) and may reflect similarities the model learned between related diseases such as insomnia, narcolepsy and other sleep/wake disorders, not biological evidence. A wake-promoting drug could plausibly help daytime function in people with disturbed sleep, but it would not be expected to improve insomnia itself and could even worsen it.

Overall, the link between the original and predicted indications is indirect, and the rationale is weak.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The high TxGNN score is not backed by meaningful evidence. The only completed trial is small (40 participants) with no results available, the other trial was terminated with 2 participants, and the literature does not test modafinil in insomnia. Because modafinil promotes wakefulness, it is not expected to treat insomnia itself and could worsen it.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Review of the results of the completed trial NCT00124384 (modafinil with or without CBT-I in primary insomnia)
- A clear clinical hypothesis, for example whether the goal is improving daytime function rather than treating insomnia
- Mechanism of action data
- Package insert warnings and contraindications for Indian-marketed products, to allow a safety screen
- A safety assessment for patients with cardiovascular and psychiatric comorbidities
