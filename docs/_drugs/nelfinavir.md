# Nelfinavir: From HIV Infection to Simian Immunodeficiency Virus Infection

## One-Sentence Summary

Nelfinavir is an HIV-1 protease inhibitor originally used to treat HIV infection and AIDS.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) infection**,
but there are **no registered clinical trials** and only **3 publications** (all in vitro or indirect), so the evidence is preclinical at best.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Nelfinavir is an HIV-1 protease inhibitor. Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, nelfinavir belongs to the protease inhibitor class and its efficacy in HIV infection is established.

SIV is a closely related lentivirus with a homologous protease, so a protease-inhibition rationale is plausible. The retrieved literature consists of in vitro susceptibility work on HIV-2, SIV and SHIV against anti-HIV-1 compounds. This is indirect, preclinical evidence only.

The high score mostly reflects similarity to the original HIV indication rather than a new therapeutic opportunity. SIV infection is a non-human primate model, not a human clinical indication.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The only support is indirect in vitro work, with no clinical trials. SIV infection is an animal model rather than a human indication, so the high TxGNN score reflects similarity to HIV rather than a new opportunity. The other predictions are weaker still: feline AIDS is a veterinary indication with no evidence, a rare neurodevelopmental disorder has no clear mechanistic link, and obsolete familial combined hyperlipidemia points toward possible harm, since protease inhibitors are known to cause dyslipidemia.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Indian regulator, to enable safety screening
- Detailed mechanism of action data (MOA)
- Identification of a human-relevant indication, for example by reviewing other predictions or established off-target effects of nelfinavir
- Direct nelfinavir susceptibility data in SIV or relevant models, if this direction is pursued
