# Glycine: From Allergic Rhinitis and Related Upper Respiratory Conditions to Nasal Cavity Disease

## One-Sentence Summary

Glycine is an amino acid. The knowledge base used for this report lists its original indications as upper respiratory conditions such as allergic rhinitis, rhinitis, pharyngitis, common cold and nasopharyngitis. The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **nasal cavity disease**, but the evidence is very thin: **1 clinical trial** (judged incidental) and **2 publications** (neither tests glycine as a treatment). This is essentially a model prediction only.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available. Based on known information, glycine is a simple amino acid already marketed in India as an oral tablet and as an irrigation solution. Its listed original indications are upper respiratory conditions, and mechanistically it may be applicable to nasal cavity disease, but the data compiled for this report do not support a direct mechanistic link.

"Nasal cavity disease" is a broad category that overlaps heavily with the existing indications (allergic rhinitis, rhinitis, nasopharyngitis). The high score is therefore likely the model extrapolating within the same disease family rather than identifying a genuinely new use. The predicted indication is not a clear repurposing opportunity.

A second prediction, **acute laryngopharyngitis** (score 99.84%), has no trials or literature at all. It is closely related to the existing pharyngitis, common cold and nasopharyngitis indications, and it is likewise a model extrapolation rather than independent evidence of efficacy.

## Safety Considerations

The knowledge base lists the following diseases as contraindications for glycine. These come from knowledge-graph relationships, not from package insert text:

- **Kidney and urinary conditions:** kidney disease, chronic kidney disease, chronic renal failure, end stage renal failure, nephrolithiasis, nephrocalcinosis, acute urate nephropathy, pyoureter
- **Calcium, phosphorus and parathyroid disorders:** hypercalcemia, humoral hypercalcemia of malignancy, hyperparathyroidism, osteitis fibrosa, hyperphosphatemia, phosphorus metabolism disease, hypervitaminosis, sarcoidosis, lytic metastatic bone lesion
- **Cardiovascular conditions:** heart disease, cardiovascular disease, arteriosclerosis obliterans, digitalis poisoning, ventricular fibrillation
- **Metabolic and other:** phenylketonuria, disorders of phenylalanine metabolism, constipation disorder

No drug interaction records were found. Please refer to the package insert for further safety information.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The prediction rests only on a model score. The single trial found is an unrelated imaging study, the two publications do not test glycine, and the predicted indication largely overlaps with existing upper respiratory uses.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Mechanism of action data for glycine, to assess a plausible link to nasal cavity disease
- Approved indication text and package insert warnings and contraindications for the India-marketed products
- Clinical or preclinical studies that actually test glycine in nasal or laryngopharyngeal conditions
- Clarification of whether nasal cavity disease is truly distinct from the existing labelled uses
- Route compatibility assessment (oral and irrigation forms are available; the route needed for the new indication is not yet defined)
