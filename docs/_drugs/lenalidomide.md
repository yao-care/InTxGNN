## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data for lenalidomide is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on general knowledge, lenalidomide is a cereblon E3 ligase modulator. It promotes degradation of the transcription factors IKZF1 and IKZF3, and of CK1alpha, which matters in del(5q) myeloid disease. It also activates NK and T cells.

Its efficacy in plasma cell myeloma and myelodysplastic syndrome is established. Myelodysplastic syndrome is closely related to acute myeloid leukemia, because it carries a risk of transforming into it. The CK1alpha-degradation mechanism gives a plausible reason for activity in del(5q) AML and MDS. The immune-activating effects also support its use as maintenance or combination therapy. Most of the trials listed below test exactly these settings.

Other predicted indications are weaker. Unclassified MDS has only cohort and case-report literature. Refractory cytopenia of childhood has a single pediatric Phase 1 study. Aregenerative anemia is supported mainly by myeloma and lymphoma papers that are not relevant to it. Severe congenital hypochromic anemia with ringed sideroblasts has no studies at all. Del(5q) has a strong biological rationale but is a cytogenetic finding, not a stand-alone clinical indication.

## Safety Considerations

- **Disease-level contraindication flags (knowledge graph, not label text)**: thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, agranulocytosis, pulmonary embolism, impaired renal function, cancer, embryoma.
- **Drug Interactions**: 347 interactions were recorded. Major-level interactions include **rosuvastatin** and **simvastatin**. Moderate-level interactions include **eliglustat** and **naltrexone**. Many others have an unspecified severity level.

Please refer to the package insert for warnings and label contraindications, which were not available for this report.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Several completed Phase 2 trials, including single-agent studies in AML and maintenance studies, support activity in myeloid leukemia. Lenalidomide is already marketed in India. However, the trials are small and mostly single-arm, there is no completed Phase 3 randomized trial, and the published literature is mainly reviews. The supporting data therefore remain exploratory, and the highest-risk populations (older patients, cytopenias, renal impairment) need safeguards.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Indian package insert warnings and contraindications, which are a blocking gap for safety screening
- Confirmed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Results from the randomized Phase 2/3 and Phase 3 trials (NCT01522976, NCT04490707), and a focused review of the randomized evidence in AML
- A safety plan covering myelosuppression, thromboembolism, renal dosing, and the statin interactions
- Definition of the target population, such as del(5q) disease, post-transplant maintenance, or combination with azacitidine
