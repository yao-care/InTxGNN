## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Bicalutamide blocks the androgen receptor. Androgen signaling drives hair follicle activity, so blocking the receptor could plausibly reduce androgen-dependent hair growth. Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report, so this reasoning rests on the drug's known class.

The one publication found is a comment on a retrospective review of 35 patients, which examined bicalutamide for minoxidil-induced hypertrichosis in female pattern hair loss. That suggests clinical data may exist. However, the comment is not primary evidence, and the data compiled for this report do not include the underlying study or its results.
