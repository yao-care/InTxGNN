## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Clofarabine is a second-generation purine nucleoside analog. It inhibits ribonucleotide reductase and DNA polymerase, which depletes the nucleotide pool needed for DNA replication, and it induces apoptosis through mitochondrial pathways. Detailed mechanism-of-action data are not available in the data compiled for this report, so this description rests on established pharmacology described in the cited reviews.

These mechanisms act on rapidly dividing leukemic cells, whether of lymphoid or myeloid origin. This makes it plausible that activity seen in lymphoblastic leukemia extends to myeloid leukemia. Reviews on clofarabine in acute myeloid leukemia (AML) describe single-agent activity and good tolerability in combinations, particularly in older adults and in relapsed or refractory disease.

Direct clinical evidence is substantial. It includes a completed Phase 3 randomized trial in older adults with newly diagnosed AML (n=727) and a completed double-blind Phase 3 trial of clofarabine plus cytarabine versus cytarabine alone in relapsed or refractory AML (n=326), along with many Phase 1 and 2 studies.

## Safety Considerations

- **Conditions of concern**: Clofarabine is flagged as a concern in patients with liver disease, gallbladder disease, kidney failure, thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, agranulocytosis, anemia, hyperbilirubinemia (including Gilbert syndrome), hypotensive disorder, capillary leak syndrome, and active infections (including fungal infections). These are not taken from label text and should be confirmed against the package insert.
- **Drug Interactions**: 466 interactions were identified. The 20 listed are all moderate, including acarbose, pioglitazone, amphotericin B (including lipid, cholesteryl sulfate and liposomal forms), bupropion, mesalazine, balsalazide, clarithromycin, clotrimazole, kanamycin, levofloxacin, minocycline, naltrexone, neomycin, nicotinamide, nitisinone and olsalazine.
- **Published case**: Kidney toxicity has been reported in an adult with refractory AML (PMID 24081220).

Package insert warnings and contraindications for the Indian products were not available; please refer to the package insert.
