## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known information, vildagliptin is a dipeptidyl peptidase-4 (DPP-4) inhibitor. It prolongs the activity of incretin hormones (GLP-1 and GIP), and its efficacy in type 2 diabetes is established. Pharmacology data list DPP-4 as its main target, with additional binding to DPP-8, DPP-9 and TRPV4.

A mechanistic link to focal stiff limb syndrome is **not supported** by the available data. This condition belongs to the stiff-person spectrum, which is driven by autoimmunity and impaired GABAergic signalling. DPP-4 inhibition has no established connection to these pathways. The high TxGNN score is most likely a computational artefact of network proximity, not evidence of a therapeutic effect. No similarity analysis between the original and predicted indications has been completed.

## Conclusion and Next Steps
