# Clomipramine: From Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder to Anxiety Disorder

## One-Sentence Summary

Clomipramine is a tricyclic antidepressant with a strong serotonin reuptake effect, originally used to treat obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD).
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **anxiety disorder**, with **8 registered clinical trials** and **20 publications** compiled for this prediction.
Most of the trials are OCD-focused, so the strongest direct support lies in panic disorder and agoraphobia, which are covered in the literature.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Based on known pharmacology, clomipramine is a potent serotonin reuptake inhibitor. Its active metabolite, desmethylclomipramine, adds noradrenergic activity.

OCD was historically grouped with anxiety disorders, and serotonergic modulation overlaps with the pathophysiology of panic and other anxiety disorders. Network meta-analyses and the WFSBP guidelines support clomipramine use in panic disorder.

A caveat: the registered trials supplied are mostly OCD-focused (augmentation studies, pediatric SRI partial responders, IV vs oral dosing). Direct randomized evidence in a non-OCD anxiety disorder therefore comes mainly from the published literature rather than the trial registry.

## Safety Considerations

- **Disease-level contraindication signals (knowledge graph, not label text):** epilepsy, manic bipolar affective disorder, liver disease, gallbladder disease, substance abuse/dependence, malignant hyperthermia, pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma, glaucoma, hyperthyroidism (Graves disease, thyrotoxicosis), neuroblastoma, and several cancers (duodenum, small intestine, heart, adrenal gland).
- **Drug interactions:** 318 known interactions in total. Major-level examples include epinephrine, bupropion, lorcaserin, potassium citrate and cisapride. Moderate-level examples include famotidine, cimetidine, clarithromycin, morphine, acetylsalicylic acid, and several anticholinergics (atropine, hyoscyamine, glycopyrronium, clidinium).
- **Literature-reported adverse events:** pancytopenia (case report), tic induction (tourettism, case report), sexual dysfunction, and anticholinergic effects.

Please refer to the package insert for label warnings and contraindications.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Clomipramine has a long record in panic disorder and agoraphobia, supported by network meta-analyses, guideline documents and several controlled studies, and is widely marketed in India. However, the registered trials are mainly in OCD, and the safety profile is substantial.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the Indian regulator
- Mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Confirmation of the approved indications for the registered products
- Modern randomized trial evidence for the specific anxiety disorder subtype being targeted
- A safety plan covering cardiac, seizure, anticholinergic and serotonergic risks, the high number of interactions, and CYP2D6/CYP2C19 variability
