## India Market Information

Sisomicin has 11 valid single-ingredient registrations in India. The approved indication text is not available for any of them. Main registrations:

| Authorization Number | Product Name | Dosage Form | Approved Indication |
|---------|------|------|-----------|
| 202463 | Sisorest Cream | Cream, 15 g tube | Not available |
| 212736 | Sisoptin 10mg Injection | Injection, 1 ml vial | Not available |
| 211094 | Sisocin Eye Drop | Eye drop, 3 ml | Not available |
| 210618 | Sisoptin 50mg Injection | Injection, 1 ml vial | Not available |
| 209890 | Sisowill 0.1% Cream | Cream, 10 g tube | Not available |

The remaining six registrations are Ensamycin eye drop and creams, Sisocin creams and Sisonext cream. The available forms are topical, injectable and ophthalmic.

## Safety Considerations

Please refer to the package insert for safety information.

Aminoglycosides as a class are associated with ototoxicity and nephrotoxicity.
