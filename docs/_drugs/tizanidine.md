## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism of action data is not available in the data compiled for this report. Tizanidine is a central alpha-2 adrenergic agonist. It is thought to reduce migraine frequency by modulating trigeminal and brainstem pain transmission, and by lowering neck and head muscle tension, which may contribute to chronic daily headache.

The strongest direct clinical evidence comes from chronic daily headache rather than episodic migraine. A double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicentre trial tested tizanidine as add-on preventive therapy in chronic daily headache, a group that includes chronic migraine (PMID 12167135). Several reviews also list tizanidine among preventive options for chronic migraine and chronic daily headache. A Phase 3 trial in episodic migraine prevention is registered, but no results are available yet.

## Safety Considerations

- **Drug Interactions**: 249 interactions are recorded. Major-level interactions include famotidine, cimetidine, morphine, dolasetron and obeticholic acid. Moderate-level examples include clarithromycin, levofloxacin, metoclopramide, atropine and naltrexone. CYP1A2 inhibitors such as fluvoxamine and ciprofloxacin deserve particular attention.
- **Other concerns**: Sedation, low blood pressure and liver toxicity risk call for monitoring. Liver failure, kidney disease, psychotic and other mental disorders, and hypotensive disorders are listed as conditions of concern for this drug.

Please also refer to the package insert, as label warnings and contraindications are not available in the data compiled for this report.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
A randomized placebo-controlled trial in chronic daily headache (most participants had migraine) and a registered Phase 3 migraine prevention trial support the prediction (Evidence Level L2). However, the Phase 3 trial has no verified results, and the best direct evidence is in chronic daily headache rather than episodic migraine.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Results from the Phase 3 trial NCT05484349 in episodic migraine
- Package insert warnings and contraindications
- Detailed mechanism of action data
- A safety plan covering sedation, hypotension, liver monitoring and CYP1A2-related interactions
