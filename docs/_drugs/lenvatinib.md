## Lenvatinib: From Renal Cell Carcinoma and Thyroid Cancer to Liposarcoma

## One-Sentence Summary

Lenvatinib is an oral multi-kinase inhibitor originally used to treat renal cell carcinoma and thyroid carcinoma.
The TxGNN model predicts it may be effective for **liposarcoma**,
with **1 completed clinical trial** (a single-arm Phase Ib/II study) and **4 publications** currently supporting this direction, though only one of them reports clinical results.

## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Lenvatinib inhibits VEGFR1-3, FGFR1-4, PDGFRα, KIT and RET. These receptors drive tumour angiogenesis and growth-factor signalling, which are also relevant in soft tissue sarcomas. The data compiled for this report do not include a formal mechanism-of-action entry, so this description comes from the mechanistic rationale in the prediction record.

Renal cell carcinoma and thyroid carcinoma are both highly vascular tumours that respond to anti-angiogenic therapy. Liposarcoma is a different tumour type, but angiogenic and growth-factor pathways may also contribute to its growth. Eribulin, a microtubule inhibitor with a complementary mechanism, may add antitumour activity when combined with lenvatinib. Preclinical work also suggests synergy with agents acting on CDK4, which is amplified in well-differentiated and dedifferentiated liposarcoma.

The mechanistic argument is plausible but indirect. It is supported mainly by one small single-arm study.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
The liposarcoma evidence rests on a single small, single-arm Phase Ib/II study (n=30) of lenvatinib combined with eribulin, which also included leiomyosarcoma. This supports further research (L2) but is not enough to move ahead, and the lenvatinib-alone contribution is unknown. Safety screening is also blocked because package insert warnings and contraindications are missing.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- Package insert warnings and contraindications from the India regulatory source (currently a blocking gap)
- Detailed mechanism of action data from DrugBank
- Randomised or larger controlled data in liposarcoma, ideally separating the effect of lenvatinib from eribulin
- A safety and drug-interaction management plan, especially for QT-prolonging co-medications

**Other predictions in this evaluation:**
- **Renal carcinoma** is an already approved original indication (L1, Phase 3 CLEAR trial), so it validates the pipeline rather than being true repurposing. The suggested decision is Proceed with Guardrails.
- **Xp11.2/TFE3-fusion renal cell carcinoma** (L4) has only one not-yet-recruiting Phase 2 trial. **Unclassified renal cell carcinoma** (L3) is supported by single-arm Phase 2 data and case reports. Both are research questions.
- **Renal cell carcinoma associated with neuroblastoma, ovarian myxoid liposarcoma, childhood kidney cell carcinoma, angiolipoma, familial spontaneous pneumothorax and endocrine-cerebro-osteodysplasia syndrome** have only model predictions (L5) and no trials or literature. All are on Hold.
